FIX: WINDOWS SOUND GETS DISTORTED DURING ZOOM CALLS (Acer PurifiedVoice)
========================================================================

Symptoms:
As soon as you join a Zoom meeting (or another video-call app), all other sound
in Windows breaks: in music you only hear the highest frequencies, the sound
stutters, gets quieter, and sounds muffled or "underwater". In games you hear
almost nothing except explosions and gunshots. It happens on every output device:
laptop speakers, wired, USB and Bluetooth headphones.

Cause:
Acer PurifiedVoice, Acer's AI noise reduction. It switches itself on when a
video-call app starts, and it processes not only your microphone but also
everything that goes to your speakers. It treats music and game sound as
"noise" and cuts it out.


STEP 1. Open Acer PurifiedVoice
-------------------------------
1. Press Start (the Win key).
2. Type: PurifiedVoice
3. Open "Acer PurifiedVoice Console".

If search finds nothing, open Acer Quick Access or NitroSense and look for
PurifiedVoice / AI Noise Reduction there.


STEP 2. Turn off noise reduction
--------------------------------
In the PurifiedVoice window, switch OFF all noise reduction features:

  - AI Noise Reduction;
  - anything for speakers or incoming audio (Speaker, Playback, incoming
    voices). This is the main cause;
  - automatic activation for video-call apps, if there is such an option
    (Auto-enable / Conference apps / Video conference mode).

Option names may differ slightly between app versions.
Simple rule: anything that says Noise Reduction — turn it off.


STEP 3. Check
-------------
1. Keep Zoom running (any meeting will do) and play some music or a video.
2. The sound should be normal again: bass is back, no stuttering,
   no "underwater" effect.

If it didn't help, restart Zoom (fully quit it from the tray icon and open it
again) and check once more.


STEP 4. Noise suppression for your own voice (optional)
-------------------------------------------------------
To keep background noise out of your microphone, use Zoom's own noise
suppression. It only processes your microphone and does not touch Windows sound:

  Zoom → Settings → Audio → Background noise suppression → Auto or High.


IF THE DISTORTION COMES BACK
----------------------------
Acer updates (Acer Care Center, Windows Update, driver updates) sometimes turn
PurifiedVoice back on. If the symptoms return, repeat steps 1–3.

The radical option is to uninstall the app:
  Start → Settings → Apps → Installed apps →
  Acer PurifiedVoice Console → "..." → Uninstall.
You can reinstall it later from the Microsoft Store or Acer's support site.


WHAT WAS CHECKED AND IS NOT THE CAUSE
-------------------------------------
  - The microphone Zoom keeps open while muted: fully disabling it in Windows
    did not help.
  - Windows communications ducking (Control Panel → Sound → Communications →
    "Do nothing"): did not help.
  - Speaker "Audio enhancements" and DTS:X Ultra: did not help.

Tested on: Acer Nitro ANV15-52, Windows 11, Zoom Workplace 6.7,
Acer PurifiedVoice Console 2.0.14.
